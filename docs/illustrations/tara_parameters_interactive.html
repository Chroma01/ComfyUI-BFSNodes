Exact rule

For time difference Δt: penalty = min(c, λ × (max(0, |Δt| − w) / f)²). The relative multiplier is exp(−penalty). At the defaults and Δt=0.4 seconds, the penalty is1.28 and the multiplier is about0.278. Other logits and attention normalization still determine the final probabilities.
